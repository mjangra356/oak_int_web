Eligibility Criteria

  • ✓ Students must meet the age requirements for their respective grades by September 1st.
  • ✓ Previous academic records and transfer certificates are mandatory for grades 2 and above.
  • ✓ An entrance assessment may be required for middle and high school placements.
  • ✓ International students must provide equivalent and translated documentation.

Admission Process

1

Submit Application

Complete the online application form and pay the non-refundable registration fee.

2

Document Review

Submit all required documents including transcripts, birth certificate, and ID proofs.

3

Assessment & Interview

Shortlisted candidates will be invited for an interactive session or grade-level assessment.

4

Enrollment Decision

Offer letters are sent out. Secure your spot by paying the admission fee on time.
